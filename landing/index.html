Transparent Decision Reasoning & Underlying Math

FORMULA VERIFICATION
STEP 01
Stock Coverage Runway

660 units ÷ 60 units/day = 11.0 days

Current available stock will be exhausted in exactly 11 calendar days at the observed baseline rate.

STEP 02
Planning Review Threshold

8 days (lead) + 5 days (buffer) = 13.0 days

To maintain a 5-day safety cushion upon new stock arrival, review must trigger whenever runway hits 13 days.

STEP 03
Deficit & Decision Window

11.0 days < 13.0 days → 2-day deficit

If a purchase order is submitted today, goods arrive on Day 8, leaving 3 days of stock remaining—2 days under your 5-day buffer target.

Assumptions & Order Quantities: Baseline demand is modeled at 60 units/day. Supplier lead time is fixed at 8 days. Required order sizing is intentionally not forced here: batch quantities (MOQ), case packs, and target forward weeks should be chosen by the inventory planner. Before you upload,
here’s what to know.

Straight answers about the data Titan needs, how forecasts are made and what you can take away.

What data do I need to start?

A sales history CSV up to 50 MB. Typical sales fields include a date, product SKU, quantity sold and unit price; category may also be supported. The upload flow helps validate and map your columns.

How are forecasts made?

Titan trains XGBoost models on your history and explains each forecast with SHAP, so you can see how seasonality, promotions and lead time move the number. The forecast view can include a confidence interval when available.

How far ahead can it forecast?

The forecast view shows a 12-week horizon, with uncertainty illustrated alongside the prediction.

What does Risk Radar flag?

Demand spikes, drops and forecast misses. It compares each day with a rolling baseline using z-score detection and highlights signals for investigation and possible follow-up.

Can I test a decision before making it?

Yes. Scenario Lab simulates a promotion, a price cut or a supplier delay, then shows the effect on demand, stockout risk and the order you would need to place.

What can I export?

Reports as CSV, Excel and PDF.

Is the data on this page real?

No. Every number here comes from simulated supply-chain transactions, used only to show how Titan works.
